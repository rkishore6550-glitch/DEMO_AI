How these are measured

  • Lead to login uses the lead created time and the login time. Leads that logged in before the lead was created count as logged in but are left out of the time.
  • Login to conversion counts converted leads that logged in, out of all logged-in leads. Converted leads with no login are added to the total and shown separately.
  • The conversion date has no time, so login to conversion is in whole days. Conversions dated before the login day are left out of the time.
  • Time to first attempt and first connect run from the lead created time to the AI's first call and first connected call. Leads created at night wait for the morning, which pulls the average up, so the median is shown too.

How to read this

  • Every lead here had at least one AI call from the demo bot, filtered by the bot's agent ID, and is in the agent data, so the AI called it and it is back with an agent.
  • "AI connected" means at least one connected AI call. A lead with no campaign name is one the AI never connected. Those leads are kept as a separate flow.
  • "What the AI did" uses the AI call flags: a demo scheduled first, then a follow-up (callback requested), otherwise conveyed only.
  • "Agent called" means any agent call at any time, and "agent spoke" means at least one connected agent call from any agent, not just the assigned one. The data has no connect times, so a connected call could in theory come after the lead converted.
  • The Flow, AI connected, How they converted and AI not connected tabs count leads. A lead is in exactly one row of the converted split.
  • The demo date and the converted date are as recorded on the lead. I do not know whether the demo date is when the demo was booked or given.
  • The time between the AI call and the first agent call is not shown, because the AI and agent times in this data are on different clocks. The conversion timing check uses whole days only.
  • — phones with — AI calls are in the AI data but not in the agent data, so they are left out.
  • Revenue is the revenue without GST recorded on converted leads.
